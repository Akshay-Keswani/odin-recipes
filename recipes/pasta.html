VEGETABLE PASTA

Description

Vegetable Pasta is a simple and delicious dish made with pasta, fresh vegetables, and flavorful sauces. It combines soft, cooked pasta with vegetables like onions, tomatoes, and other colorful veggies for a tasty and satisfying meal.

It is quick and easy to prepare, making it a great choice for lunch or dinner. The pasta can be enjoyed on its own or served with a side of salad or garlic bread.

Ingredients Required:

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon chilli flakes
  • salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons pasta sauce

Steps to make vegetable pasta:

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until soft, then drain it.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the chopped onion and vegetables.
  3. Add the tomato, pasta sauce, chili flakes, and salt.
  4. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything well.
  5. Cook for 2–3 minutes and serve hot.
