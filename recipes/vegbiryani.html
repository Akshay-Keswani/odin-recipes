VEG BIRYANI

Description

Veg Biryani is a flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, fresh vegetables, and a blend of delicious spices. It is cooked with ingredients like carrots, peas, beans, potatoes, onions, and tomatoes, giving the dish a colorful and tasty combination.

This vegetarian version of biryani is perfect for a satisfying lunch or dinner. Served with raita or a side salad, veg biryani makes a wholesome and delicious meal that is loved by people of all ages.

Ingredients Required:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables(carrot,peas,beans and potato)
  • 1 large onion , thinly sliced
  • 1 tomato ,chopped
  • 2-3 green chillies , chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 1-2 cardamom pods
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
    • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon biryani masala
  • salt to taste
  • 3-4 cups of water
  • fresh corriander leaves
  • fresh mint leaves
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • A few stands of saffron(optional)

Steps to make Veg Biryani

  1. Wash and soak the rice – Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Prepare the vegetables –Wash and chop the vegetables such as carrots, peas, beans, and potatoes into small pieces.
  3. Cook the spices -Heat oil or ghee in a large pot. Add cumin seeds, bay leaf, cloves, cardamom, and cinnamon. Sauté for a few seconds until fragrant.
  4. Cook the onions –Add the sliced onions and cook until they turn golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, then sauté for another minute.
  5. Add vegetables and spices –Add the chopped tomatoes and mixed vegetables. Add turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala, biryani masala, and salt. Mix everything well and cook for a few minutes.
  6. Add rice and water –Drain the soaked rice and add it to the pot. Gently mix it with the vegetables. Add the required amount of water and yogurt.
  7. Cook the biryani –Cover the pot and cook on low to medium heat until the rice is completely cooked and the water has been absorbed.
  8. Garnish and serve –Turn off the heat and let the biryani rest for 5 minutes. Garnish with fresh coriander and mint leaves. Serve hot with raita or salad.
